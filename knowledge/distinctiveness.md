# Distinctive, not arbitrary

## Banishing the "Generic AI Template"

AI-generated websites fail most often because they converge on the same exhausted template regardless of whether the prompt asks for a Kyoto architecture practice, an indie film festival, or a Rust database engine.

**Never generate these Generic AI Template anti-patterns:**
- Centered hero with a pill badge above a purple-to-cyan gradient headline (`"Revolutionize / Supercharge / Transform your..."`) and two generic buttons over a blurry radial glow.
- Three or four identical rounded cards in a row, each with a tiny icon in a tinted circle, a two-word bold title (`"Fast Performance"`, `"Secure by Default"`, `"Easy Integration"`), and a two-line platitude.
- Uniform bento grids filled with decorative filler widgets that communicate no real product or domain mechanic.
- Default `system-ui` / `Arial` / `sans-serif` typography paired with raw `#ffffff` or `#000000` backgrounds and Tailwind-default `#3b82f6` blue buttons.
- Placeholder copy (`Lorem ipsum`, `"Project Title"`, `"Description goes here"`, `"Company A / Company B"`) or bureaucratic disclaimers explaining that real data was not provided.

## How to create authentic distinctiveness

Distinctiveness comes from **deep commitment to the subject's domain**, expressed through structure, typography, materiality, and concrete specificity:

1. **Pass the Substitution Test:** Replace the brand name with a competitor or an unrelated company. If the hero headline, section titles, and visual motif still work unchanged, rewrite and restructure until they could only belong to this specific subject.
2. **Author Concrete Domain Specificity:**
   - *For an existing repo:* Surface actual domain entities, real configuration syntax, real operational states, and verified product capabilities.
   - *For a new site / short prompt:* Write vivid, domain-literate copy and specifications. An architecture studio site should name real materials (*board-formed concrete, weathered Corten steel, Hinoki cypress*), spatial programs (*courtyard residence, adaptive reuse gallery*), site metrics (*640 m², completed Autumn 2025*), and design philosophy. A fintech or AI launch page should show concrete transaction flows, latency distributions, or inspectable prompt/response traces.
3. **Commit to a Signature Structural Motif:** Choose one cohesive visual/structural device that anchors the identity across the page:
   - *Indexical Monograph Ledger:* Numbered chapters (`01`–`05`), exposed `1px` architectural grid lines, sticky specification rail, and large-format imagery/SVG schematics.
   - *Editorial Broadsheet:* Asymmetric multi-column layout, high-contrast serif headlines, pull-quote interruptions, and topical index tables.
   - *Interactive Artifact Stage:* A live, manipulable component (spec configurator, filterable project matrix, interactive timeline, or code/preview split) that proves the concept by letting the visitor use it immediately.
4. **Preserve Usability on Conventional Controls:** Put creative expression into typography, composition, surface atmosphere, and domain artifacts—never into confusing navigation labels, inaccessible custom scrollbars, or hidden primary actions.

## Originality audit checklist

1. **Category truth:** Does the page immediately look and feel like a flagship example of its specific category rather than a generic SaaS template?
2. **Concrete copy:** Is every headline, label, and data point specific to the domain, with zero generic slogans or placeholder text?
3. **Pacing variety:** Do consecutive sections differ in layout structure, density, and visual weight?
4. **Typographic voice:** Would the page still feel designed and authoritative if all photographs were hidden, purely from its typography, spacing, and grid hairlines?
5. **Resilience:** Does the distinctive layout adapt cleanly to narrow mobile viewports, `200%` zoom, keyboard navigation, and `prefers-reduced-motion`?
