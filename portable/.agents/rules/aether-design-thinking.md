# AETHER design entry workflow

For design, UI/UX, frontend, layout, styling, or visual implementation tasks:
1. From the project root, read `.agents/skills/aether-design-thinking/SKILL.md` first and follow its design-thinking workflow.
2. Read `aether/INDEX.md` to find the relevant AETHER guidance and assets.
3. Use that guidance while planning and implementing the task; do not skip the entry workflow.
