# Anti-generic design

Use on every build or redesign to prevent interchangeable, template-looking AI output. Read [distinctiveness](../../../aether/knowledge/distinctiveness.md), [visual direction](../../../aether/knowledge/visual-direction.md), and [category directions](../../../aether/knowledge/category-directions.md).

1. **Reject the "Generic AI Template":** Never ship the default AI layout: a centered purple/blue gradient-text hero (`"Transform your workflow"`), two generic pill buttons, a row of fake logo placeholders, 3 identical icon-in-a-circle feature cards, a uniform bento grid, flat `#ffffff` or `#000000` backgrounds, and `system-ui` everywhere.
2. **Run the brand substitution test:** If you swapped the brand name with a random competitor or another industry and the layout, copy, and visual rhythm still made sense, the design is too generic. Anchor the page in **domain-specific mechanics, vocabulary, and artifacts**.
3. **Replace placeholders with domain-authentic specificity:**
   - **Existing product/repo with real data:** Extract actual workflows, CLI commands, domain constraints, and verified proof from the codebase; never invent fake legal/compliance claims.
   - **New website / short prompt:** Author **concrete, realistic, domain-authentic content**—e.g., specific project titles (*"Karuizawa Pavilion — Nagano, 2025"*), material palettes (*board-formed cedar, poured basalt, brushed brass*), exact dimensions (*420 m²*), or realistic product interactive states. **Never** output `Lorem ipsum`, `"Feature 1, Feature 2"`, `"Your tagline here"`, or barebones placeholder disclaimers.
4. **Introduce a signature structural motif:** Give the design at least one memorable, subject-relevant structural signature—such as an architectural spec ledger with tabular numerals, an interactive before/after comparison, an editorial split narrative with sticky index, or a bespoke SVG technical schematic—while keeping navigation and interactive controls clear, keyboard-operable, and responsive.
