# Category-aware creative directions

These are **creative direction lenses, not rigid templates**. Every category demands a distinct relationship between **typography, spatial rhythm, surface palette, information density, and hero architecture**. Never flatten an architecture monograph, cultural publication, or luxury flagship into a generic SaaS landing page, and never drop marketing fluff into a high-density workbench.

## Deep category lenses

### 1. Luxury Architecture, Interior & Industrial Design Studio
- **Visual personality:** Sculptural, tactile, unhurried, museum-grade restraint.
- **Typography:** High-contrast editorial serif display (`Cormorant Garamond`, `Instrument Serif`, `Newsreader`, `Playfair Display`) paired with a quiet geometric/neo-grotesk sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, `DM Sans`) and tabular monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, `IBM Plex Mono`) for index numbers (`01 / 04`), site coordinates, floor area (`480 m²`), and completion years.
- **Surface palette:** Warm architectural neutrals—travertine, limestone, alabaster, raw linen (`#F6F3EE`, `#EDE8DF`), deep charcoal bronze (`#141311`), and muted graphite hairlines (`rgba(20, 19, 17, 0.12)`) with a single mineral accent (oxidized bronze, warm terracotta, or aged brass).
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** Asymmetric editorial hero with monumental photography or architectural elevation SVG, generous negative space (35–45% open canvas), indexical project ledger, split sticky monograph narratives, and material palette specimens.
- **Failure to avoid:** Centered SaaS gradient headlines, 3-column Lucide icon feature cards, pill badges, or sterile unstyled white boxes.

### 2. SaaS Marketing & Developer/AI Product Launch
- **Visual personality:** Crisp, high-velocity, technical precision, unmistakable product proof.
- **Typography:** Authoritative grotesk or technical display (`Space Grotesk`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Syne`, `Inter`) with tight display tracking (`-0.03em`), paired with clean UI body text and crisp monospace for live code, telemetry, or command palettes.
- **Surface palette:** Multi-tiered atmospheric dark (`#090A0F` canvas → `#11131C` surface-1 → `#181B26` surface-2) or high-craft technical paper (`#F8FAFC` with slate hairlines), subtle 1px specular borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), and a disciplined high-contrast accent.
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** Split or theater-style hero featuring an interactive or hyper-detailed product artifact (annotated workflow diagram, live terminal/query inspector, interactive state switcher) beside a specific value proposition; followed by an asymmetric architectural breakdown, real workflow sequence, and transparent specification table.
- **Failure to avoid:** Vague `"Supercharge your workflow with AI"` copy over a blurry purple blob followed by three identical icon cards.

### 3. SaaS Workbench, Dashboard & Admin Application
- **Visual personality:** Calm operational clarity, high scannability, zero decorative bloat.
- **Typography:** Scannable UI sans (`Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or platform UI stack where native feel is required) with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` enforced on all metrics, timestamps, deltas, and tables.
- **Surface palette:** Low-fatigue layered neutrals with clear elevation hierarchy, crisp `1px` structural dividers, and semantic status tokens (`success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`) always paired with icons and text labels.
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** No marketing hero. Open directly into the primary operational workspace: summary KPI strip with context/deltas, filterable primary data view or canvas, keyboard-accessible command bar, and predictable detail drawers.
- **Failure to avoid:** Giant marketing banners, oversized display serifs, or decorative scroll effects inside an operational tool.

### 4. Editorial, Magazine & Long-Form Cultural Publication
- **Visual personality:** Expressive typographic voice, literary pacing, print-inspired authority.
- **Typography:** Expressive serif headlines and pull quotes with dramatic scale contrast, comfortable `60–68ch` prose measure, generous line height (`1.6–1.75`), drop caps or section kickers, and crisp metadata sidebars.
- **Surface palette:** Warm newsprint, parchment, or ink-on-cream (`#FAF7F2` / `#121212`) with crisp editorial rules (`1px solid` and `3px double` or bold section bars) and restrained rubric accents.
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** Lead story feature with strong art direction, asymmetric multi-column grid with sidebar marginalia, pull-quote interruptions, and curated topic indexes.
- **Failure to avoid:** Edge-to-edge 120-character paragraphs, uniform card grids that treat essays like e-commerce SKUs, or tiny low-contrast body text.

### 5. E-Commerce Flagship & Physical Product Brand
- **Visual personality:** Sensory, tactile, desire-driven yet frictionless at purchase.
- **Typography:** Distinctive brand display voice paired with ultra-clear specification, price, and sizing typography (`tabular-nums`).
- **Surface palette:** Product-framing neutral canvas (warm stone, gallery white, or rich espresso/obsidian) that makes product materiality pop without competing hues.
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** Tactile hero showcasing product form and materiality, immediate price/variant/stock clarity, close-up craft callouts, specification matrix, transparent shipping/returns, and an effortless cart/checkout flow.
- **Failure to avoid:** Hiding price, variant selection, or Add-to-Cart behind scroll-jacking animations or vague lifestyle collages.

### 6. Creative Agency & Design/Engineering Portfolio
- **Visual personality:** Opinionated point of view, curatorial confidence, proof through shipped work.
- **Typography:** Bold typographic tension (e.g., monumental grotesk or editorial serif paired with technical index metadata).
- **Surface palette:** High-contrast gallery surfaces with deliberate theme shifts or project-specific accent framing.
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** Statement thesis + immediate curated project index or flagship case study showcase detailing role, constraints, architecture, and measurable outcome—never a generic "Our Services" 3-card template.
- **Failure to avoid:** Mystery-meat navigation that hides work/contact, or a wall of thumbnails with zero context on what was actually built.

### 7. Cultural Institution, Exhibition, Festival & Entertainment
- **Visual personality:** Atmospheric, event-driven, poster-like graphic conviction with effortless wayfinding.
- **Typography:** Expressive display hierarchy (condensed grotesk, kinetic display, or exhibition serif) paired with structured schedule, venue, and ticket tables.
- **Surface palette:** Rich stage/gallery contrast with bold thematic accents and high-legibility overlays.
- **Spatial rhythm & hero:** Poster-scale exhibition/program identity with immediate dates, location, and booking entry; program lineup matrix; spatial venue guide; and accessible media previews (never autoplay audio).
- **Failure to avoid:** Unreadable text over busy background video or burying dates, hours, and tickets beneath heavy effects.

## Cross-category synthesis

When a brief blends categories (e.g., *editorial e-commerce* or *architectural real-estate platform*), combine their **functional tasks and structural strengths**—such as editorial storytelling chapters leading into a rigorous interactive specification and booking flow. Test the resulting concept against the substitution check in [distinctiveness](distinctiveness.md) and the 13-dimension gate in [resources and QA](resources-qa.md).
